# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes sales performance using the Global Superstore dataset. It provides business insights into sales, profit, customer performance, product categories, and regional trends through interactive visualizations.

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

To help business stakeholders monitor key sales metrics, identify high-performing products and customers, and understand sales performance across different regions.

---

# 📈 Key KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin (%)

---

# 📊 Dashboard Features

- Yearly Sales Trend
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Top 10 Customers by Sales
- Interactive Slicers
  - Category
  - Region
  - Segment

---

# 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- SQL
- Microsoft Excel

---

# 📂 Dataset

Global Superstore Sales Dataset

# 💡 Key Insights

- West region generated the highest sales.
- Technology was the highest-selling category.
- Profit margins varied significantly across product categories.
- A small group of customers contributed a significant share of total sales.
- Interactive filters enable quick business analysis across regions, categories, and customer segments.
